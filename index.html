Installing

  1. Open Forge Setup (Windows).exe from your downloads. If the browser says it isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep.
  2. Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”. That's because Forge isn't from a big company with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click through the installer. Forge goes on your desktop and in the Start menu, and opens when it's done.
  1. Open Forge (Mac).dmg from your downloads and drag Forge onto Applications.
  2. Open Forge from Applications. The Mac says it can't verify Forge, because it isn't from a paid Apple developer account. Click Done (not Move to Trash).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Forge and click Open Anyway, then enter your password. You only do this once.
If the Mac says Forge is damaged, open Terminal and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Forge.app, then open Forge again.

Your code

  1. When Forge opens for the first time it asks for a code. Use the one Psilocybian sent you; it looks like FORGE-XXXX-XXXX-…
  2. Paste it under Or enter a code and click Use code.
  3. That's it. Forge keeps itself up to date from now on. The code works on one computer: on a new one, Forge asks Psilocybian to move it.
